## Case Studies

### 01. Automated ProForma Generation Process

`UiPath` · `SAP` · `Orchestrator Queues`

Ten analysts generated daily ProForma invoices in SAP by hand — repetitive, easy to forget, and occasionally triggering wrong "order shipped" notices to customers. I automated the full flow and designed a smart reprocessing logic for blocked deliveries so nothing is forgotten and nothing is sent early.

**Impact** — 308+ hours saved per year · manual work eliminated · validated by the Global RPA Team

📄 `01-automated-proforma-generation-process`

---

### 02. Advance PIX Payment Automation

`Power Automate` · `SharePoint` · `Teams`

Payment requests were sent one by one, with no standard and no paper trail. I built a Microsoft 365 flow that standardizes the request, attaches the ProForma, and runs an automatic D+1 follow-up with an interactive card in Teams.

**Impact** — standardized billing · full traceability · automatic payment follow-up

📄 `02-advance-pix-payment-automation`

---

### 03. Active Customer Database & BI Platform

`UiPath` · `Power BI` · `Excel`

The South America active-customer base was pulled from SAP by hand every week. I automated the extraction, built a standardized history, and turned it into geographic and operational dashboards for Master Data.

**Impact** — always-current customer base · self-service geo/ops analytics

📄 `03-active-customer-database-analytics`

---

### 04. Customer Status Verification Database

`Data` · `Automation`

A structured solution to check automatically whether a customer is active or inactive, replacing manual lookups and supporting faster operational decisions.

**Impact** — automated status verification

📄 `04-customer-status-verification-database`

---

### 05. Contilink Password Reset Automation

`Microsoft Forms` · `Power Automate`

Resetting a Contilink password and emailing credentials was fully manual. I replaced it with a form that dispatches the login to the customer automatically.

**Impact** — repetitive manual communication eliminated · faster turnaround

📄 `05-contilink-password-reset-automation`

---

### 06. Shipping Condition Transition Automation

`UiPath` · `SAP (ZSDZBOA)`

Changing the shipping condition on every BRCB order was done order by order. I automated the mass transition through SAP, removing the manual touch entirely.

**Impact** — bulk order updates with zero manual effort

📄 `06-shipping-condition-transition-automation`
